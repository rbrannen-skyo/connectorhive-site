You found us in your logs. That's the point.

ConnectorHive-Crawler collects publicly posted self-storage pricing and availability. We could do that without a name attached. We'd rather you know we're here, and be able to do something about it.

ConnectorHive-Crawler/1.0 (+https://connectorhive.com/crawler; crawler@connectorhive.com)

Never rotates. One rule matches every request.
Deployed by a person. No collector starts itself.

We collect

  • Posted prices and promotions
  • Unit sizes, types, availability
  • Location, hours, listed features

We never touch

  • Anything behind a login
  • Personal information
  • Reservations or checkouts
  • CAPTCHAs, limits, or blocks

Set the terms in robots.txt

Read before every run. Changes apply on the next pass.

User-agent: ConnectorHive-Crawler
Crawl-delay: 30
User-agent: ConnectorHive-Crawler
Disallow: /

Replace / with a path to limit us rather than stop us.

We read only what a visitor sees without an account. Our practices are reviewed by counsel — see hiQ Labs v. LinkedIn (9th Cir. 2022), Meta v. Bright Data (N.D. Cal. 2024), Van Buren v. United States (2021) for context, not clearance.
